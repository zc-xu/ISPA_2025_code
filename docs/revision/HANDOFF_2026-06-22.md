## 5. 当前 CLS 初始化敏感性结果

论文主图建议使用：

- `output/pdf/cls_init_sensitivity_130_heatmap.pdf`
- `output/png/cls_init_sensitivity_130_heatmap.png`

辅助图可使用：

- `output/pdf/cls_init_random_vs_greedy_10_150.pdf`
- `output/png/cls_init_random_vs_greedy_10_150.png`

精简结果表：

| Config | Random mean gap | Random CV | Best deterministic init | Worst gap | Greedy gap |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| `10_130` | 2.10% | 4.53% | Density | 2.10% | 0.00% |
| `10_150` | 1.27% | 2.56% | Density | 15.88% | 15.88% |
| `10_180` | 2.40% | 5.88% | Density | 2.40% | 0.00% |
| `5_130` | 0.00% | 0.00% | Density | 0.00% | 0.00% |
| `15_130` | 0.00% | 0.00% | Density | 0.00% | 0.00% |
| `20_130` | 0.00% | 0.00% | Density | 0.00% | 0.00% |

建议写法：

> We further evaluate the initialization sensitivity of CLS by comparing random initialization with four deterministic initialization strategies. All strategies are followed by the same CLS local-search procedure. The results show that CLS is generally robust to initialization: under the fixed 130-user setting, most server-scale cases converge to the same final transmission cost, and only the 10-server case shows a small random-initialization gap. In addition, the marginal greedy initialization performs worse in the 10/150 case, indicating that a seemingly stronger greedy initialization may also be trapped in a poorer local optimum.

中文含义：

> 我们不是证明 random 永远最好，而是证明 CLS 经过局部搜索后整体对初始化不敏感。固定 130 用户规模时，大部分服务器规模下不同初始化都收敛到相同最终传输成本；10/130 中 random 只有小幅 gap。同时 10/150 的辅助结果说明单纯边际贪心不一定更好，可能陷入更差局部最优。因此选择 random 是简洁、稳定且不引入额外偏置的方案。

## 6. 当前 Pareto 指标结果

10/130 representative case：

| Method | HV, higher better | IGD, lower better | Best Q, lower better |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| NS-P | 0.8191 | 0.0785 | 0.3894 |
| GCP | 0.8596 | 0.0492 | 0.3550 |
| GDP | 0.8945 | 0.0326 | 0.3363 |
| PSP | 0.9470 | 0.0016 | 0.3282 |

可用结论：在该代表性规模下，PSP 在 HV、IGD、Best Q 三个主指标上均优于原有初始化策略。HV 越高越好；IGD 和 Best Q 越低越好。
