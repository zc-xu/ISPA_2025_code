## 2. Final experiment design

Two complementary evaluations are retained:

1. **Alternate real region for the main manuscript**
   - configuration: `real_sparse_r04_c40_u130_k10_s1`;
   - 40 real candidate base stations, 130 users, 10 selected servers, and 8 services;
   - candidate-station centroid is 24.20 km from the original Xizhimen centroid;
   - full Stage I and Stage II results over seeds 42, 43, and 44.

2. **Expanded-region stress test for the response or supplementary material**
   - common topology: 40 real candidate stations and 10 selected servers;
   - 130 users under sparse, clustered, and skewed traffic;
   - user footprint is 2.25--2.88 times the original area;
   - Stage II uses population 50, 200 generations, and seeds 42, 43, and 44.

The station coordinates are real. The new heterogeneous traffic profiles are reproducibly generated and must not be described as measured population distributions.

## 3. Validated results

### Alternate real region

- Stage I CLS cost: `2304.7670`;
- best recorded non-CLS baseline: `6150.5741`;
- CLS reduction: `62.5276%`.

Three-seed Stage II means:

| Method | HV | IGD | Best Q |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| NS-P | 0.9574 | 0.0498 | 0.2953 |
| GCP | 0.9799 | 0.0446 | 0.2800 |
| GDP | 0.9742 | 0.0408 | 0.2733 |
| PSP | **1.0116** | **0.0129** | **0.2678** |

PSP has the best mean HV, IGD, and Best Q. Its mean HV is 3.23% higher than the strongest competing mean, IGD is 68.28% lower, and Best Q is 1.99% lower.

### Expanded-region stress test

| Profile | Area ratio | Density CV | CLS reduction | Best Stage II mean method |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Sparse | 2.88 | 0.5409 | 26.63% | GDP |
| Clustered | 2.25 | 0.5343 | 34.82% | GCP |
| Skewed | 2.44 | 0.6001 | 51.54% | GCP |

All 36 method/profile/seed records contain 50 valid nondominated solutions. PSP is not uniformly best in the expanded stress test. The defensible conclusion is that the two-stage workflow remains applicable and CLS is robust in Stage I, while Stage II initialization rankings are distribution dependent.

## 7. Reproduction

After pulling the branch, regenerate the summary tables and figures from the committed evidence:

```powershell
.\LocalSearch\Scripts\python.exe .\LocalSearch\reviewer6_generalization_summary.py
```

To rerun Stage II from the committed expanded-region datasets:

```powershell
$configs = @(
  'real_review6_large_c40_u130_k10_sparse_r00_c40_u130_k10_s0',
  'real_review6_large_c40_u130_k10_clustered_r00_c40_u130_k10_s0',
  'real_review6_large_c40_u130_k10_skewed_r00_c40_u130_k10_s0'
)
foreach ($seed in 42,43,44) {
  .\LocalSearch\Scripts\python.exe .\LocalSearch\run_real_region_stage2.py `
    --screen-csv output\csv\real_region_stage1_screen_review6_large_c40_u130_k10.csv `
    --configs $configs `
    --pop-size 50 `
    --n-gen 200 `
    --seed $seed `
    --output-prefix "review6_large_region_stage2_seed$seed"
}
```

Full regeneration of the real-station subsets requires the external protected station-pool workbook. The exact Stage I command is recorded in `reviewer6_geographical_generalization_response.md`.
