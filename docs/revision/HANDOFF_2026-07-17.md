## 2. Final experiment design

The retained end-to-end evaluation uses configuration `real_sparse_r04_c40_u130_k10_s1`:

- 40 real candidate base stations, 130 users, 10 selected servers, and eight services;
- candidate-station centroid 24.20 km from the original Xizhimen centroid;
- candidate-station count doubled from 20 to 40;
- coverage-density coefficient of variation increased from 0.2336 to 0.3359;
- complete Stage I and Stage II results over seeds 42, 43, and 44;
- five preference-conditioned DQN policies per seed, compared through the common equal-weight Best Q metric.

The station coordinates are real. The workload is generated with fixed seeds under the same protocol as the original experiment.

## 3. Validated results

- Stage I CLS objective: `2304.7670`;
- best recorded non-CLS initialization-only objective: `6150.5741`;
- CLS reduction: `62.5276%`.

Three-seed Stage II means:

| Method | HV | IGD | Best Q |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| NS-P | 0.9574 | 0.0498 | 0.2953 |
| GCP | 0.9799 | 0.0446 | 0.2800 |
| GDP | 0.9742 | 0.0408 | 0.2733 |
| PSP | **1.0116** | **0.0129** | **0.2678** |

PSP has the best mean HV, IGD, and Best Q. Its mean HV is 3.23% higher than the strongest competing mean, IGD is 68.28% lower, and Best Q is 1.99% lower.

DQN returns five preference-conditioned solutions per seed. Under the same per-seed normalization bounds and the common `Q = 0.5 x normalized cost + 0.5 x normalized delay` definition, its mean Best Q is `0.5517 +/- 0.1021`; PSP obtains `0.2678 +/- 0.0503`, which is 51.45% lower.
