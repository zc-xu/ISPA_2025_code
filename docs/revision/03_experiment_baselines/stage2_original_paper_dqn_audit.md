# Stage-II Original-Paper Results and DQN Audit

## Final decision

The two Stage-II scale sweeps use the values stored in the original paper workbook as the authoritative four-method results. The submitted main-run seed is `42`. DQN is added as a fifth method from the saved seed-42 objective outputs.

No seed search is used to select favorable outcomes. The paper workbook is fixed first, and the code/history audit is used only to identify its protocol and reconstruct the DQN comparison.

## Evidence chain

1. The active seed in the submitted `main.py` and `LocalSearch/nsga_service_deploy.py` is `42`.
2. Seed `44` belongs to later sensitivity and multi-seed checks.
3. For `10_130`, the submitted seed-42 protocol reproduces all four historical `F` arrays element by element; the maximum absolute difference is zero.
4. Recomputed `10_130` Best-Q values differ from the original Excel display by at most `4.1e-5`, consistent with four-decimal rounding.
5. Seed-20 and seed-44 checks for `10_150` do not jointly reproduce the four archived bars and are therefore not treated as the original main-run source.
